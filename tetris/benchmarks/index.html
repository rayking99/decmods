Method, accuracy definition, and limits

32 frozen boards × 2 option-order permutations = 64 trials per model, plus two seeded games. The deck contains 12 observed gameplay positions, 12 reference-policy positions, and 8 controlled challenges.

Accuracy here means agreement with this explicitly supplied one-step rule: minimize holes; then maximize lines cleared; then minimize peak height, total height, and roughness. Every tied best move counts. This is a proxy for following a Tetris policy, not proof of optimal long-term play. Non-dominated moves are also recorded in the raw results.

All three models receive the same compact description and priority rule. One opening position is tested separately using the original verbose demo format; this is a compatibility probe, not a full game validation of that format. Models run one at a time, after warmup. Cold start is excluded from the resident-model table; prompt-cache effects can still affect latency. The entire placement includes all tournament calls when there are more than 26 legal moves.

Hardware: Apple M3 Max, 128 GB. Ollama 0.35.0. The benchmark stores model digests, frozen inputs, all raw requests and responses, trial errors, and complete game traces.
